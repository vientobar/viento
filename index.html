Our Story

A place to slow down
& taste with intention.

Viento is not in a rush — and neither should you be. Nestled among olive trees on the Pelješac coast with Korčula shimmering across the channel, it’s a place built for lingering. For that second coffee. For a brunch that turns into an afternoon.

Everything here is made with care. Our signature cocktails are crafted from scratch — homemade syrups, fresh ingredients, nothing off the shelf. Our coffee is taken seriously. Our matcha is premium.

Come as you are. The wind, the view, and something delicious are waiting.
