Our Story

A space shaped by good energy & the joy of taking things slowly.

Located just steps from the sea, Viento is your go-to spot for calm mornings, golden hour drinks and everything in between.

We opened Viento because we wanted a place we couldn’t find anywhere else. A creative outlet built around flavours we genuinely love making — cocktails crafted from scratch, coffee taken seriously, homemade iced teas and flavoured lemonades.

The wind sets the mood at Viento — so much so, it made it into the name. Viento, Spanish for wind, blows here daily, cooling the terrace and turning it into an escape from the Adriatic heat. With a little help from the shade of the olive trees.

This is our second season, and the cocktails our guests loved most last summer are staying exactly where they belong — in the Hall of Fame. Including our Not So Slim Shady, the one that made people stop and ask: wait, is that olive oil? It is. And it works.

And for those who like the familiar done differently — our Classic Twists take the cocktails you already love and give them a Viento touch. Same soul, our way.

Everything here is made with care and intention; soft music, calm vibes and thoughtful flavours.

Come and see for yourself. The wind, the view, and something delicious are waiting.
